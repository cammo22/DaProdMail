# **App Name**: DaProdMail

## Core Features:

- Email Configuration: Allow configuration of email settings (Google Mail with app password).
- Customer Addition: Add individual customers with their email addresses to the application.
- Customer Categorization: Categorize customers into different groups.
- Email Composition and Sending: Compose and send emails to individual customers or entire categories of customers.
- Email Improvement Tool: Utilize a generative AI tool (using the Google API key) to improve the text content of the emails before sending.
- Email Status Notifications: Confirmation and error messages displayed during email sending to keep the user informed of progress.

## Style Guidelines:

- Primary color: Deep blue (#3F51B5), providing a professional and trustworthy feel for business communications.
- Background color: Very light blue (#E8EAF6), offering a clean and non-distracting backdrop to ensure readability.
- Accent color: Yellow-orange (#FFAB40), used for interactive elements and important call-to-actions to draw attention.
- Clean and professional sans-serif font for all text elements.
- Use clear and consistent icons to represent customer categories and email functions.
- Use a straightforward and intuitive layout for managing customers and composing emails.
